# Gospel  

## John 2.1-12

On the third day there was a wedding in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there. Jesus and his disciples had also been invited to the wedding.  
  
When the wine gave out, the mother of Jesus said to him, “They have no wine.” And Jesus said to her, “Woman, what concern is that to you and to me? My hour has not yet come.” His mother said to the servants, “Do whatever he tells you.”  
  
Now standing there were six stone water jars for the Jewish rites of purification, each holding about a hundred litres. Jesus said to the servants, “Fill the jars with water.” And they filled them up to the brim. He said to them, “Now draw some out, and take it to the chief steward.” So they took it.  
  
When the steward tasted the water that had become wine, and did not know where it came from (though the servants who had drawn the water knew), the steward called the bridegroom and said to him, “Everyone serves the good wine first, and then the inferior wine after the guests have become drunk. But you have kept the good wine until now.”  
  
Jesus did this, the first of his signs, in Cana of Galilee, and revealed his glory; and his disciples believed in him. After this he went down to Capernaum with his mother, his brothers, and his disciples; and they remained there a few days.


# Homily


- As I started 2025, like I usually do most years, I took some time to look backwards, reflecting on what 2024 was like for me.
	- I looked backwards to what I thought 2024 would be like and compared that to how it actually was
	- And I have come to realize that it is too easy to get caught up in the idea of what life should look like
	- In reality though, life can be messy, unpredictable and yet, at the same time, sometimes it turns out way better than we could ever plan for!
	- Now, isn't that an interesting perspective!
	- Despite how we want our life to unfold,  life often just doesn't work that way
	- It can be messy and unpredictable and when you think about it, maybe that's what makes life beautiful!
	- 2024 was definitely a year of change for me.
	- Some opportunities came my way that I hadn't anticipated.
	- And embracing those opportunities definitely required me to step outside my comfort zone, to take a chance and try something different
- And sometimes, although we know that trying something new could be exciting and benefit us, often, we actually have a fear of change
- a fear that prevents us from wanting to trying something new
	- Maybe it's a fear of failure
	- or a fear of being judged by others
	- or maybe it's just that the predictability of staying in our comfort zone reduces the stress and anxiety in our life, so we stay the course
	- Regardless of the reason though, sometimes, we need to make changes in our life to be able to move forward, to embark on the path we are meant to follow
- Trying something new is not always easy, especially if we think it will be challenging
	- but some things I have learned over the years is this:
		- We can't gain wisdom without the experiences
		- We can't gain strength without the struggle
		- We can't get results without the effort
		- If you want results, you have to do the work
		- There are no shortcuts
		- And through doing the work, this is where you learn the most!

- In today's Gospel, Jesus has come to a point of major change in His journey here on earth
	- Jesus performs His first miracle, the first of the signs that reveals His divine authority and power
	- demonstrating that he was more than just a prophet or teacher
		- water is transformed into wine, a symbol of joy and abundance
		- but this sign is not simply about wine
		- it's about something much deeper
	- The miracle at Cana was a sign of how when we turn to Jesus with our problems, He guides us towards abundance
	- towards transformation in ourselves when we trust in His guidance

- Mary turned to Jesus and said "They have no wine" and told the servants to "do whatever He tells you"
	- Mary, the Mother of God sees the problem, that the wine was running out, a potentially embarrassing situation for the Bride and Groom
		- She points the servants to Jesus
		- To listen to Him and to follow His instructions
		- She knew that Jesus would help solve this problem
		- the servants obeyed Jesus's instructions even though they probably seemed pointless or even absurd
			- they obeyed and witnessed a miracle emphasizing the importance of trusting in God in the face of the unknown
		- and this is the essence of our discipleship
		- we bring our needs, our concerns, our burdens to Jesus
		- and if we take the time to listen to Him, to see the signs and guidance He gives us, then we can find the answers we are looking for

- Jesus's first miracle, this first Messianic sign is just the beginning of Him becoming more Public in His ministry
	- it represents a change in the way that Jesus carries His ministry forward
	- His visibility in the public eye will increase from this point onward
	- He will be more well-known among the people
	- It also suggests that Jesus is embracing His messianic role and using His power and abilities for the benefit of others and for our own salvation
	- That's a big change from the life He had been living up to this point
	- Gone is the quiet life of a carpenter's son that He had been living before

- One of the interesting things about signs, is that you only notice them if you are present and paying attention.
	- Have you ever been on a drive or journey somewhere and a good song comes on the radio or you are chatting with someone in the car and miss the sign for a turn off you are supposed to take?
	- Sometimes the signs of what to do in our lives are there, but we are just not paying attention to them and end up missing them
	- Don't worry though, God is patient and will continue to send the signs we need to see

- Or maybe we see the signs, but our fears of making changes stop us from trying something new or embracing change
	- this is where trusting and having faith in God's plan for our lives,
	- through reflecting on the signs that we see
	- can help us to identify the choices we need to make
	- Our faith can strengthen us and give us the hope that God's plan will give us the meaning and purpose we are seeking in our lives
	- Now hope doesn't always mean that you know how things will pan out
	- Because, when you think about it, maybe God's got a better plan for us than our own plan :-)

- At the start of 2024, I had a plan for the year and I had hopes that those plans would get me where I needed to be
	- However, sometimes hope is more about having the belief that one day things will get better
	- And by the end of 2024, I can say that while life did not unfold according to the plan that I had for my life, I am happy with how 2024 ended up unfolding
	- And it did take some work on my part, to see the signs that a change was needed
	- to decide to make changes, to revise my plans as the year progressed
	- As I said earlier, getting results requires effort.
	- You don't get the results without putting in the work
	- take time for reflection and prayer
	- assess if you need to change something in your life
	- look back to see if the choices you have made in the past have given you the results you had hoped for
	- or maybe you need to make some changes
	- engaging in prayer gives us the chance to get in touch with our deepest, most real and honest version of ourselves, as only God can reveal to us

- so as we prepare to celebrate the Eucharist, let us think
	- that with faith
	- patience
	- and prayer
	- we can open ourselves to see the changes we need to make in our lives, 
	- to have the strength to make those changes, one choice at a time
	- to overcome our fears to continually remake ourselves into the person that Jesus knows we can be
