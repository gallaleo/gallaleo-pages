# Gospel
## Matthew 1.18-24 of Sunday December 21, 2025

The birth of Jesus the Christ took place in this way. When his mother Mary had been engaged to Joseph, but before they lived together, she was found to be with child from the Holy Spirit. Her husband Joseph, being a righteous man and unwilling to expose her to public disgrace, planned to dismiss her quietly.  
  
But just when he had resolved to do this, an Angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, “Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife, for the child conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit. She will bear a son, and you are to name him Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins.”  
  
All this took place to fulfill what had been spoken by the Lord through the Prophet: “Look, the virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and they shall name him Emmanuel,” which means, “God is with us.” When Joseph awoke from sleep, he did as the Angel of the Lord commanded him; he took her as his wife.


# Homily

- Every holiday has a history, and every history carries a meaning
	- and the 4 weeks of Advent is a season where we prepare ourselves for Christmas
	- we await in joyful hope for the coming of God our Savior
	- and later this week on Christmas day, we will hear some of the history that gives Christmas meaning

- Hopefully we have all taken some time to slow down and reflect
	- time to think about what meaning Christmas bears in our own lives this year
	- slowing down may not always be an easy thing at this time of year
	- the last few weeks have been busy for me in many ways, work, ministry, and the day to day things that need to be taken care of in my life
	- and reflection doesn't really seem to happen when we are rushing around trying to do everything
	- reflection only happens when we slow down or when something slows us down
	- but it is important!!
	- because reflection brings us back to our center, not because we find answers, but because we have stopped rushing
	- when you are not used to it, slowing down can feel uncomfortable
	- and remember, as we are told in the Book of Genesis, even God took a day of rest on the 7th day
	- Slowness is the only way you actually feel your life.  It's part of how God made us!
		- rushing blinds you
		- speed erases memory
		- the brain doesn't record at the speed of fast!!
		- To feel your life, you need to slow it down and be there for it
		- be present!
	- Not everything that we do is important or needs to be done right away
	- so hopefully, you can set aside some time for reflection, time to slow down
	- time to think about what meaning Christmas has in your life this year

- Today's Gospel provides us with a reminder that many of us may need to hear at this time of year
	- especially if these last few days leading up to Christmas are proving to be busier than we would like
	- that reminder is that 'God is with us'!
- In the Gospel, it says that:
	- All this took place to fulfil what had been spoken by the Lord through the Prophet: “Look, the virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and they shall name him Emmanuel,” which means, “God is with us.”
	- Christmas is the fulfilment of God's promise to be intimately present with His people.
	- The name Jesus means "God saves" and the name Emmanuel means "God is with us"
	- Jesus was born and lived among us here on earth
		- to bring God's presence to us, and to provide us with a path to salvation

- I have a question for you to think about
	- as you look back through the years, has the meaning of Christmas changed for you?
	- I have to think that maybe it has
	- I know for me, it has
	- the things that were important for me about Christmas as a child or even a teenager are very different than the things that were important for me as a parent of young children
	- and those things that were important for me as a parent of young children are different for me now that I am a parent of adult children, and now that I am a Deacon
	- I have to think that the meaning of Christmas for each of us changes over time just as our lives change over time

- One of the important meanings of Christmas that stays the same though is that it is a time to celebrate the birth of Jesus
	- and a time to thank Him for His life, His sacrifice and His Resurrection
	- Jesus is the meaning of Christmas
	- and for me, this brings a sense of peace that I often do not feel at other times of the year

- A lot can change in a year, since last Christmas
	- maybe there has been a new addition to your family
		- maybe a new child, or a new son-in-law or daughter-in-law
	- maybe you have gotten a new job or moved to a new place
	- or maybe you have lost someone this year and this is your first Christmas without them
	- all of these things can change the meaning of Christmas for us
	- and maybe we also change our traditions because of these changes

- but one of the things that hasn't changed is that God is always with us
	- we just have to slow down enough to let Him into our lives
	- to make room for Him in our day to day

- In the Gospel, we see God at work in the lives of Mary and Joseph
	- Mary has been found to be with child from the Holy Spirit
	- and Joseph, upon learning that Mary was with child and it was not his, he was filled with conflict
	- to save her from public shame and potentially death under the Law, he decides to divorce her quietly
	- but in a dream, God has sent an Angel to Joseph, to assure him that God is with him and that the child is from the Holy Spirit and that Joseph must name Him Jesus
	- God does not leave us alone in our times of challenge

- Our faith enables us to see God in all things around us and to see the Holy Spirit at work in our lives
	- Our deeds allow others to see our faith in action
	- and it is through our deeds that we can find meaning in our lives

- So, as we prepare to celebrate the Eucharist on this 4th Sunday of Advent
- let us remember
	- that the meaning of Christmas can change for us over time
	- that we often have to slow down and reflect to be able to see these changes
	- and that regardless of the changes in our lives, God is always with us
